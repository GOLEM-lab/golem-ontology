01 — THE PROJECT

A shared conceptual ground for stories.

Stories are more than collections of characters and events. Their meaning emerges from the relationships between their components and the contexts in which they unfold.

GOLEM develops a formal, domain-independent model of narrative and fiction. By making narrative concepts and relationships explicit, the ontology supports consistent knowledge representation and computational analysis.

Its modular approach aims to provide a reusable library of narrative components, including characters, events, settings, social relationships, and narrative inference. This creates a foundation for comparative research across texts, genres, and cultural contexts.

04 — RESEARCH CONTEXT

From narrative theory to computable knowledge.

GOLEM connects formal ontology engineering with literary studies, digital humanities, and computational approaches to cultural evolution. Its conceptualization treats narratives as structured systems whose components can be studied in relation to one another.
