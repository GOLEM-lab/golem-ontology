

## Updating proposal for `golem:G16_Object` scope definition and restrictions

```turtle
# old
rdfs:comment "An object refers to a persistent item that plays a significant role in the story but is not a character."@en ;
# new (to implement)
rdfs:comment "An object refers to a persistent item that has a narrative significance in the story and, although it may also play narrative roles, is distinct from a character."@en ;
```


If we enforce the following:

```turtle
golem:G16_Object rdf:type owl:Class ;
                 rdfs:subClassOf [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;
                                   owl:onProperty dlp_extDnS:plays ;
                                   owl:someValuesFrom golem:G11_Narrative_Role
                                 ;
```

we force objects to play at least one narrative role, which it is not the case.

The only way to avoid constraining `dlp_extDnS:plays` domain and range directly, or to extending it with a specific GOLEM subclass, is to leave open the possibility of such a use of `dlp_extDnS:plays` in relation to objects.
## Updating proposal for `golem:G1_Character` restrictions

Right now we have the following:

```turtle
golem:G1_Character rdf:type owl:Class ;
                   rdfs:subClassOf ecrm:E28_Conceptual_Object ,
                                   dlp_extDnS:agentive-social-object ,
                                   [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;
                                     owl:onProperty dlp_extDnS:involved-in ;
                                     owl:someValuesFrom golem:G4_Social_Relationship
                                   ] ,
                                   [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;
                                     owl:onProperty dlp_extDnS:plays ;
                                     owl:someValuesFrom golem:G11_Narrative_Role
                                   ] ,
                                   [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;
                                     owl:onProperty dlp_extDnS:plays ;
                                     owl:someValuesFrom golem:G6_Relationship_Role
                                   ] ,
                                   [ rdf:type owl:Restriction ;
                                     owl:onProperty dlp_extDnS:uses ;
                                     owl:someValuesFrom golem:G16_Object
                                   ] ;
```


- A character may be involved in a social relationship. Right now we impose the existence of at least one instance of social relationship connected to a character.

	Since right now the scope note of our use of `dlp_extDnS:involved-in` target characters and social relationships only, a solution may be replacing the `owl:someValuesFrom` with `owl:allValuesFrom`, avoiding existential commitment over such a relationship.

- The same reasoning applies to relationship role: a character may play a relationship role only if it is involved in a social relationship, which it is not always the case. 

	I would drop the restriction in this case.
