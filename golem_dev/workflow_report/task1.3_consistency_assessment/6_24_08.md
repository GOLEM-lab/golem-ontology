

## Versioning solution

Incrementing to a **Major** version is logically required because changing an ontology's formal semantics (restrictions and axioms)—even while preserving the existing vocabulary names—alters its logical entailment and breaks backward compatibility (see [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/) and [W3C Member Submission](https://www.w3.org/submissions/2024/SUBM-semantic-versioning-20241127/)). Version compatibility fundamentally means that an importing system will not be harmed by switching versions; altering axioms risks introducing semantic contradictions or untyped resources into active data pipelines.
